Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 • Compliant with Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023

1. Scope and Applicability

FinTrack Technologies ("we", "our", or "us") provides a software-as-a-service platform for finance businesses, NBFCs, and chit funds to manage lending operations. This policy governs how personal data of our users and their borrower clients is processed.

2. Data We Collect

We process information submitted by registered lending companies, including:

  • Business account information: Business name, owner name, email address, phone number, office address.
  • Borrower information uploaded by lenders: Name, contact number, KYC document numbers, loan account details, repayment history.
  • Technical data: IP addresses, access logs, device data for audit logging.

3. Multi-Tenant Data Isolation

All client data is partitioned using multi-tenant architecture. Your company's borrower records and collection logs are strictly isolated and never shared with or accessible by other companies.

4. Rights under the DPDP Act 2023

Data principals are entitled to know what personal data is processed, seek correction or erasure of inaccurate records, and withdraw consent where applicable. Direct all privacy requests to privacy@fintrack.in.
