Efficiency & Accuracy

Still tracking daily EMI in Excel and notebooks?

Spreadsheets work when you have 15 loans. At 100+ loans, it leads to missing cash, untracked defaults, and reconciliation errors.

The Manual & Spreadsheet Way
  • Scattered files: Every agent maintains separate books. Evening reconciliation takes 2 hours.
  • Unnoticed defaults: Overdue loans are caught weeks later when borrowers have already defaulted.
  • No cash audit: Difficult to verify how much cash an agent collected versus what was handed over.
The FinTrack Platform Way
  • One Live Cloud Ledger: Field collections instantly update the office dashboard in real-time.
  • Automated Overdue Flagging: Real-time defaulter registers calculate daily penalty fees automatically.
  • Single-Tap Agent Collection: Large mobile buttons, thermal Bluetooth receipts, and WhatsApp confirmations.

Lending Models

Built for Standard Indian Lending Structures

From daily market collection to monthly business and gold loans, schedules calculate accurately out of the box.

Daily Collection Loans

e.g., 50 or 100 days micro-loans. Collect fixed daily EMI. Configure automatic Sunday exclusion to match local market rules.

Weekly Collection Loans

e.g., 10, 15, or 20 weeks. Ideal for vegetable and market vendors with weekly installment schedules.

Monthly EMI Loans

Standard equal monthly installments (Principal + Interest) over 3, 6, 12, or 24 month tenures.

Monthly Interest Loans

Collect interest monthly while principal remains outstanding. Full bullet principal settled on the maturity date.

Weekly Interest Loans

Collect interest on a weekly basis, with full lump-sum principal settled at final loan closure.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is FinTrack?

FinTrack is a specialized cloud software platform designed for Indian lending businesses—including microfinance institutions, NBFCs, chit funds, and private financiers. It helps you manage borrowers, schedule daily/weekly/monthly loan installments, track field collections in real-time on mobile, and print thermal receipts.

Does FinTrack work on field agents' smartphones?

Yes. The Today's Collection sheet is mobile-first and optimized for field agents. Agents can record payments with a single tap, handle partial amounts, share WhatsApp receipts, or print via Bluetooth thermal printers.

How does Sunday skipping work for daily loans?

FinTrack includes a business-level setting to automatically skip Sundays on daily loans, automatically shifting collection dates to the next working day to align with local weekly market closures.

How much does it cost after the free trial?

You receive an unrestricted 30-day free trial with no payment details needed. Following the trial, subscription is ₹1,000 per year (+ 18% GST), payable securely online via UPI, Cards, or Netbanking using Razorpay.

Is our borrower data secure?

Yes. FinTrack implements strict multi-tenant database isolation ensuring each company only accesses its own records. All traffic is encrypted over SSL/TLS in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023.
